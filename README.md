#  Protea healthcare-application

## Users 
Doctors / Patients / Pharmacists

# Features 
Patient page:
Sign up: role
Login (email and password)
Terms and conditions
Profile 
Add details – personal details (name, surname, ID number, personal conditions, age, email address, phone number, home address, emergency contacts)
Pharmacist: prescriptions (view prescription only)
Medication reminders: (entered by doctor) – confirm button
Appointment form: (confirm appointment by docs) date, time, reason – alert (24 hours and an hour before); block off time slots that are already taken, request rescheduling
Blog: ask questions (question, category, and choose to post the question to be posted anonymously), also view different categories
Order page: will have your order history but will also link to pharmacy page to check for better prices
Pharmacy page: will have the medication, location and name, price, and the time slots the pharmacy is open
Extra features: weekly vitals – connect to external health app/smart watch
Priority level: maybe categorize them based on specific conditions (low, medium, high )
Allergies: able to specify and add

Doctors page:
Emergency response: closest hospital, paramedics
Profile: specialization, location, name of clinic, years in profession, prices for consultation, certification – switch profile from doc to patient
Appointment form – confirm, deny, or request rescheduling
	See the schedule of appointments for the day (history and future appointments)
Blog page: answer questions, create a blog, and post photos related to the blog
Medication: prescription of medication they can upload – (maybe: Can order for patient wants/critical )
Extra feature: add lab reports link to patient appointment

Pharmacists
Profile: name, surname, email, phone, pharmacy location, certification, years of work experience - switch profile from pharmacist to patient, work time
Medication page: patient and their medications (orders)
Appointment page will become the pickup page for orders
Blog: ask and answer questions

Splash screen for all users
